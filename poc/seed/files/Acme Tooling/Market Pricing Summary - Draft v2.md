# Acme Tooling Co.: Market Pricing Summary (Draft v2)

Prepared by Brightline Advisory. Draft for discussion, not for distribution.

## Scope

- 42 benchmark jobs across manufacturing, maintenance, engineering and corporate functions
- Two published pay surveys, referred to here as Survey A (industrial) and Survey B (manufacturing)
- Data aged to a 1 January effective date at 3.5% a year

## Headline findings

1. Overall, base pay sits at 96% of the market median (P50).
2. Maintenance roles lag most, at 89% of P50. The maintenance planner and CNC lead matches are weak and need client confirmation.
3. Engineering roles are at market (101% of P50).
4. Corporate roles lead slightly (104% of P50), driven by long-tenured staff.
